Junior AI Engineer

Company: Northwind Labs
Location: Karachi
Experience: 0–2 years

About the Role

Northwind Labs is looking for a Junior AI Engineer to help build and
prototype practical AI-powered applications. The ideal candidate should
have strong Python fundamentals, familiarity with modern LLM frameworks,
and an understanding of retrieval-augmented generation systems.

Requirements

- Strong Python fundamentals.
- Familiarity with at least one LLM framework, such as LangChain or
  LangGraph.
- Understanding of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), including
  chunking, embeddings, retrieval, and retrieval quality.
- Familiarity with Git and REST APIs.
- At least one shipped or completed project that can be explained
  end to end, including the problem, architecture, implementation,
  challenges, and outcome.

What We Assess

- Technical depth
- Problem decomposition
- Ability to ship working projects
- Debugging and problem-solving ability
- Communication

The role is suitable for candidates with 0–2 years of experience,
including strong students, recent graduates, and candidates with
substantial project-based experience.
